Week 5: 3D Printing

For this week's assignment I decided to make one of the pill box compartments as a prototype for my final pill box. I was especially interested in testing the hinge mechanism, since there are many ways to make hinges in 3D printing.

I ended up following this YouTube tutorial. I designed the box in Fusion using parametric design, so I can adjust the width, height, length, and wall thickness. You do have to readjust the hinge after changing a parameter, but the rest of the box updates automatically.

The hinge worked perfectly, and I'll use it in the final design since it lets me have a flat lid. I'll still need to adjust the final design to make room for the electronic mechanism, but this prototype let me prove out the general shape of the pill compartment box.

Photogrammetry Scan

I decided to scan a pecan since it's a natural object with lots of variance. I scanned it using the RealityScan app, then imported it into Rhino and deleted the faces from the desk so the mesh is clean.
